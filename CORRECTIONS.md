# Corrections policy

Corrections are first-class records in `data/corrections/`.

A correction identifies affected records, previous and corrected summaries, reason, triggering observations, original publication time, correction time, and responsible PR.

## Propagation

A correction review must locate dependent claims, events, assessments, reports, translations, map features, and snapshots. Affected public pages receive a visible notice. Translations must be regenerated from the corrected source version.

## Preservation

Do not delete a disproven or superseded record merely to make the current dataset cleaner. Preserve provenance and lifecycle state. Remove or redact content only when retention is unlawful, dangerous, malicious, or violates privacy/access obligations; document the redaction without republishing the harmful material.

## Severity

Editorial wording fixes that do not change meaning may update a draft. Any released factual, confidence, location, attribution, or translation change requires a correction record.
