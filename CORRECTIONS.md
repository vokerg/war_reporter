# Corrections policy

Corrections are first-class records.

A correction must identify:

- affected claim, event, map feature, or report;
- previous status or wording;
- corrected status or wording;
- reason for correction;
- evidence that triggered the change;
- publication and correction timestamps;
- responsible PR.

Do not delete a disproven claim unless it contains unlawful or dangerous material. Mark it `false`, `misleading`, or `obsolete`, preserve provenance, and publish a visible correction when the claim affected a released report or map.
