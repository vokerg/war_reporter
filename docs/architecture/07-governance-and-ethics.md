# Governance, legal, and ethics

## Decision rights

Schema and methodology changes require maintainer review. Source reputation changes require evidence-linked review. High-risk map publication, personal-data handling, and released corrections require explicit human approval.

## Source and subject rights

Document platform terms, access method, copyright/quotation basis, privacy impact, and removal requests. Do not treat public availability as unrestricted republication permission. Avoid unnecessary personal data and do not profile private individuals.

## Neutrality

The project seeks transparent evidence handling, not performative “balance.” Include material competing evidence without assigning equal weight to unequally supported claims. Disclose source access gaps and institutional dependencies.

## Model accountability

AI agents are contributors, not authorities. Store their role, task, PR, and deterministic inputs where practical. Do not cite an AI summary as primary evidence. Material analytical judgments remain attributable to reviewers.
