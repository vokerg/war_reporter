# Daily report — 4 August 2026

**Reporting period:** 4 August 2026, 00:00–24:00 UTC  
**Updated:** 5 August 2026, 19:25 UTC  
**Region:** Russia–Ukraine war

## Key points

- Russian and Ukrainian officials reported continued strikes, bridge damage and a territorial-control claim, but the available reporting did not establish the results.
- Reuters, AP and Rybar described drone attacks affecting several Russian regions, including reported incidents near Moscow and St Petersburg and strikes on warehouses.
- Black Sea security and disruption to Ukrainian exports remained prominent: Türkiye called for safer navigation, while Reuters reported that vessels had not entered the relevant Ukrainian port system for almost two weeks.
- Reuters verified the Kherson location of a video linked to a Ukrainian war-crime allegation, but not the date or the identity of the attacker.

## Strikes and drone activity

The Russian Ministry of Defence said strikes against Ukrainian port-related targets were continuing and claimed that Russian forces had taken control of Baksheevka. The Ukrainian General Staff reported that a road bridge and a railway bridge had been struck. These statements establish what the parties reported; the available material did not establish the resulting damage or territorial-control outcome.

Reuters, AP and Rybar reported overlapping drone incidents affecting several Russian regions. Their accounts included reported strikes on three warehouses and activity near Moscow and St Petersburg. The overlap supports the broad picture of a multi-region attack, while details of individual incidents remain uneven because some reporting drew on the same official or company sources.

Russian official updates gave air-defence totals of 320 and 304 Ukrainian unmanned aircraft intercepted or destroyed. A Ukrainian General Staff update reported 117 enemy drones shot down or suppressed. The reports do not define compatible time intervals or categories, so the figures should not be added together or directly compared.

## Black Sea navigation and exports

Reuters reported that Türkiye's Foreign Ministry called on Russia and Ukraine to improve navigational safety in the Black Sea. In a separate interview, Ukraine's agriculture minister said there was no alternative to the ports of Odesa for the export operations under discussion. Reuters also reported that no vessels had entered the relevant Ukrainian port system for almost two weeks.

Taken together, the reports indicate continued concern over maritime security and disruption to Ukrainian export routes. The underlying port and vessel-movement records were not available in the material reviewed for this report.

## Kherson video and allegation

Ukrainian officials accused Russia of a war crime in connection with a video described as showing a drone attack on a vegetable seller in Kherson. Reuters said it verified the reported Kherson location of the video.

Reuters did not verify the date. The available reporting also did not identify the attacker or establish the legal classification of the act shown in the video.

## Civil defence and drone detection

Suspilne reported that Kyiv's shelter capacity covered 56,000 people, slightly more than one percent of the city's resident population. The figure highlights a substantial gap between reported shelter capacity and the size of the population, although the underlying capacity inventory was not available for direct review.

Suspilne also quoted a Ukrainian air-defence commander saying that FPV drones are small targets that are almost never visible on radar. This is the commander's operational assessment, not a universal statement about every radar system or operating condition.

## Policy and industry

Reuters reported that an amendment to Russian financial restrictions allowed covered foreign nationals and Russia-based company branches to manage specified funds and withdraw ruble equivalents. The underlying decree was not available in the reviewed material.

Reuters also reported, following an interview with the company's chief executive, that Ukrainian company Fire Point had begun integrating European technology into a missile-defence system. A Guardian briefing attributed criticism of NATO standards and membership promises to Valerii Zaluzhnyi, but the primary speech or transcript was not available.

## What remains uncertain

The main unresolved questions concern the actual effects of the reported strikes and bridge attacks, the territorial-control claim concerning Baksheevka, comparable air-defence figures, the duration and scale of disruption at Ukrainian ports, and the date and perpetrator of the act shown in the Kherson video. Official statements and allegations are identified as such; independent verification is noted only where it materially changes how a development should be read. No earlier daily report is available for a day-to-day comparison.
